About the Community

OpenStreetMap Syria (OSM Syria) is a volunteer-run open mapping community built around OpenStreetMap, the free, editable map of the whole world.

Our story

The community started in December 2024, after the political change in Syria. It brings together Syrians inside the country and abroad who care about maps and open-source technology, around one goal: closing Syria’s geographic data gaps.

In 2026 we ran a series of mapathons as part of the ReMapping Syria project and the OSM Syria community-building grant, bringing GIS experts and people opening the map for the first time into one place.

There is no hierarchy and there are no titles: everyone is equal in tasks, activity, participation and organisation.

Community milestones

  1. December 2024 The community starts
  2. June 2025 First online meeting An introduction to OpenStreetMap with a guest from the OSM Lebanon community
  3. December 2025 First in-person meetup in Damascus
  4. March – June 2026 Six online mapathons Part of the ReMapping Syria project
  5. September 2026 Douma workshop and Damascus meetup And securing the openstreetmap.sy domain
  6. October 2026 An article about the community on the HOT website
