Community Goals

Accurate open maps aren’t a luxury. They’re infrastructure.

1

Open, accurate maps of Syria

Help build and maintain open-source maps of Syria that support humanitarian response, connectivity, urban planning, and geographic data sharing.

  • Syrian projects on the HOT Tasking Manager in the ReMapping Syria campaign
  • Data-fixing challenges on MapRoulette

2

A Syrian network for maps and geography

Expand the social network of people in Syria and the diaspora who care about maps, geography, and open technology.

  • In-person meetups in Damascus and online meetings open to everyone
  • Conversations with the global community on the OpenStreetMap forum
  • Preparing to register the community as a local organisation in Syria
3

Free training and volunteering

Organize volunteer activities and free training sessions around mapping and geospatial technology — raising the skills of participants and the community as a whole.

  • Online mapathons on the Tasking Manager, JOSM and MapRoulette
  • In-person workshops with local partners, such as the Douma city council
  • Session recordings on YouTube for anyone who missed them
