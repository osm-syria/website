Privacy

How this website counts visits, and what it never collects.

Anonymous: we never know who you are.

We only see totals, such as how many people visited the activities page this month. You can turn statistics off at any time.

Visit statistics are on by default

From your first visit, Google Analytics counts your visit anonymously, and a small bar at the bottom of the page tells you so. You can accept or reject it in the bar, or turn statistics off at any time below. If you turn them off, nothing more is sent and the website works exactly the same.

What is collected

  • The pages you view, and when
  • The website or link that brought you here
  • Your type of device, browser, operating system, screen size and language
  • Your approximate location (country and city), which Google works out from your connection
  • A random number saved in a cookie on your device, so that a return visit counts as the same visitor. It contains no name or personal details, and it expires after 6 months.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or anything you type
  • Your IP address: Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses
  • Any link between your visit and who you are: we only see totals and charts
  • Advertising data: ad features, personalised ads and Google signals are turned off, and we never sell or share the data

Who sees the data and for how long

Only the volunteers who maintain this website can see the statistics. Google Analytics is run by Google, which processes the data on its servers, possibly outside Syria; see Google’s privacy policy. The detailed data is deleted automatically after 2 months; only the totals remain.

Other services this website uses

These don’t set cookies and aren’t used to track you, but your browser connects to them to show parts of the website:

  • Google Fonts, for the website’s fonts
  • GitHub, to show the date of the last website update
  • OpenStreetMap’s map tile servers, for the map of community members on the home page
  • beforeafter.mapki.com, only when you open a before-and-after map comparison on the goals page

Links to YouTube, Google Forms, Telegram and other websites take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

The community map on the home page shows approximate locations only, rounded to about 1 km, without names. It does not show any member’s exact address.

Turn statistics off or on

You can change this at any time. Your choice is saved only in your own browser, and turning statistics off also deletes the statistics cookie.

Visit statistics are not active on this copy of the website.
