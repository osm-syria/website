Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience?

No. Mapathons usually start by showing how to create an OpenStreetMap account, then we map together on real projects.

Is it free to take part?

Yes. The community is volunteer-run, and training and activities are free.

What language are activities in?

Mostly Arabic. Some sessions are in English or have live interpretation.

I missed an activity. Is there a recording?

Most online sessions are recorded on YouTube, with links in the public community document.
